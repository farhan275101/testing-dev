FAQ

Aam Sawal Jo Har Karkhana Maalik Puchhta Hai

1. Internet band ho gaya toh kya counter ruk jayega?+
Bilkul nahi! Isme Offline IndexedDB + PWA Sync Queue laga hai. Bina net ke bhi entry aur print chalte rahenge, aur net aate hi automatic cloud par sync ho jayega.
2. Kariger, Sancha aur Rangai walon ko mobile portal kaise milta hai?+
Malik apne app se hi Kariger/Sancha/Rangai ka Mobile No. aur 4-digit PIN set karke 1 click me WhatsApp link bhej dete hain. Worker phone par link khol kar Notifications ON kar leta hai.
3. Saal badalne par purane hisab ka kya hota hai?+
1-Click Year Closing se saare Karigers ka pichla Bana/Zari/Bani aur saare Godown ka bacha stock automatic naye saal (`2027-2028`) me chala jaata hai, aur purana saal dropdown me safe rehta hai.
4. Kya munshi ko Tally seekhna padega?+
Nahi! Express Counters mein sirf Enter dabate jao aur agla box focus hota jaata hai — 15 minute me koi bhi munshi fast entry shuru kar deta hai.

📱 ID Kaise Milti Hai, Notification & Update Kaise Chalta Hai?

1
ID aur 4-Digit PIN Kaise Milti Hai? Malik apne laptop par Kariger Master me jaakar Kariger ka Mobile No. + 4-Digit PIN set karte hain aur 📲 Share on WhatsApp dabate hain — Kariger ko sidha login link + PIN mil jaata hai.
2
Kariger Mobile me Kaise Chalu Karta Hai? Kariger WhatsApp link khol kar apna Mobile + PIN daalta hai aur home screen par Add/Install karke "🔔 Allow Notifications" ON kar leta hai.
3
Notification Kab aur Kaise Jaati Hai? Jaise hi aap ya munshi laptop par Kariger Sheet ya Kariger Express se Saree, Bana, Zari ya Rokad ki nayi entry save karte hain — Supabase Trigger turant Kariger ke phone par "🧵 Aapke Khate me Nayi Entry Hui!" push bhej deta hai!
4
Live Update & 100% Security Kariger portal kholte hi server se bilkul taza Baki Mazdoori, Loom Progress (72 me se kitni baki), Bana/Zari wazan aur pichli saari entries dikhti hain. Koi bhi kariger na kisi doosre ka hisab dekh sakta hai, na entry badal sakta hai.
