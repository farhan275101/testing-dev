FAQ

Aam sawal jo har Saree & Textile Karkhana maalik puchhta hai.

1. Internet band ho gaya toh kya counter ruk jayega?+
Bilkul nahi! Isme Offline IndexedDB + PWA Sync Queue laga hai. Bina net ke bhi entry aur print chalte rahenge, aur net aate hi automatic cloud par sync ho jayega.
2. Kariger, Sancha aur Rangai walon ko mobile portal kaise milta hai?+
Malik apne app se hi Kariger/Sancha/Rangai ka Mobile No. aur 4-digit PIN set karke 1 click me WhatsApp link bhej dete hain. Worker phone par link khol kar Notifications ON kar leta hai.
3. Saal badalne par purane hisab ka kya hota hai?+
1-Click Year Closing se saare Karigers ka pichla Bana/Zari/Bani aur saare Godown ka bacha stock automatic naye saal (2027-2028) me chala jaata hai, aur purana saal dropdown me safe rehta hai.
4. Kya munshi ko Tally seekhna padega?+
Nahi! Poore software me Right-Side Live Picker aur 1-line-1-field Express Counters hain — sirf Enter dabate jao aur 15 minute me koi bhi munshi fast entry shuru kar deta hai.
